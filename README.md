# Greenmove-Logistics-OPeration-Analysis

## Project Overview
A comprehensive Logistics Operations Analytics project designed to analyze fleet performance, driver productivity, vehicle utilization, delivery efficiency, incidents, faults, and operational costs.

The project transforms logistics data into interactive dashboards that help identify operational trends, monitor fleet performance, and uncover areas where efficiency and cost management can be improved.

## ANALYTICAL QUESTIONS
1. Driver performance: On-time rates %, average MPG, top 5 drivers by revenue
2. Trucks utilization:  count of active trucks, total revenue by trucks
3. Maintenance analysis:top 5 trucks based maintenance cost,, downtime impact
5. Fuel efficiency: MPG trends, fuel cost by make
6. Customer analysis: Revenue by customer, service levels

## DATA SOURCE
TS ACADEMY CAPSTONE PROJECT

## TOOLS

EXCEL/POWER QUERY AND POWER BI

### Exploratory Data Analysis

The exploratory data analysis was conducted using Power Query and Power BI to clean, transform, explore, and analyze the logistics operations dataset.

##  Data Preparation & Cleaning

Imported and examined the logistics datasets using Power Query.

Checked for missing, duplicate, and inconsistent records.

Standardized column names and data formats.

Corrected data types to ensure accurate analysis.

Cleaned categorical fields such as incident types, locations, and driver information.

Created calculated and transformed fields required for analysis.

Prepared the cleaned dataset for analysis in Power BI.

## Exploratory Analysis

The analysis focused on understanding key areas of logistics operations, including:

Incident frequency and incident types
Vehicle damage and incident-related costs
Fault occurrences across incident categories
Geographic distribution of incidents
Driver delivery performance
Revenue generated by drivers
Completed trips
Miles covered by drivers
Fuel consumption and efficiency
On-time delivery performance
Overall fleet and operational performance

## Key Business Insights

The analysis highlights several important operational patterns:

1. A significant number of incidents are associated with DOT violations.
2. Vehicle incidents generated substantial financial costs, with total incident costs reaching approximately $2.65M.
3. Chicago, Miami, Columbus, Salt lake city and Seattle appear among the leading locations for incident activity or cost.
4. Driver performance varies across on-time delivery, revenue generation, completed trips, fuel consumption, and mileage.
5. The 44.62% on-time delivery rate indicates an opportunity to investigate factors affecting delivery timeliness.
6. Fuel consumption is concentrated among a small group of high-usage drivers, providing an opportunity for fuel-efficiency monitoring and fleet optimization.

 ## RECOMMENDATIONS 

1. Improve On-Time Delivery Performance
Analyze the causes of late deliveries by driver, route, location, trip duration, and operational conditions. Set measurable delivery targets and provide targeted support or training for drivers with consistently low on-time performance.

2. Prioritize DOT Violation Reduction
Implement regular compliance checks, driver safety training, vehicle inspections, and preventive maintenance programs to reduce DOT-related incidents.

3. Strengthen Preventive Vehicle Maintenance
Introduce a proactive maintenance schedule based on vehicle usage, mileage, fault history, and inspection results rather than relying primarily on repairs after failures occur.

4. Investigate High-Risk Locations
Conduct location-specific analysis to determine whether route conditions, traffic, operational practices, or other factors are contributing to incidents and costs in these areas.

5.  Recognize High-Performing Drivers
Use these performance results to identify effective operating practices and share them through driver training, coaching, and performance-development programs.
